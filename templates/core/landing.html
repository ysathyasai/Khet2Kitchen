Operational Simplicity

How Farm-to-Kitchen Works

Designed for ground-level usability in rural panchayats with Vernacular Indic Voice AI.

1

Village Micro-Hub Drop

Farmers harvest and bring produce to a local aggregation center within 15 km—leveraging PACS and AIF godowns without expensive travel.

2

8-Second AI Optical Scan

Produce rolls through our standardized grading chute. Computer vision categorizes Grade A/B/C with complete color and defect analysis.

3

T+0 Payout & Cold Transit

The digital wallet credits the farmer immediately. B2B orders are dispatched in 4°C solar reefer EVs directly to urban kitchens.
